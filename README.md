# ❤️ Three of Us — A Friendship Story

> **Lahari • Renu • Varshini**

A small interactive friendship website created to celebrate the memories, bond, chaos, and beautiful moments shared between three friends.

This website tells our story in a simple cinematic flow — starting with Renu and Varshini's friendship, then bringing Lahari into the story, and finally bringing all three of us together. ❤️
